# The dashboard

The dashboard is where a responder works. The menu is grouped into three jobs: **Respond** (what
needs a person), **Observe** (what the systems are doing), and **Configure** (what the platform may
use). The incident workspace opens from any of them.

![The dashboard](../assets/screenshots/overview-light.png#only-light)
![The dashboard](../assets/screenshots/overview-dark.png#only-dark)

Every page here only reads. The platform holds no credential that can change anything you have
connected, and nothing you see is visible to another organisation using the same deployment. See
[Connect your tools](../connectors/index.md) and [Authentication](../operate/auth.md).

Two pages sit outside the menu. The [incident workspace](incident-workspace.md) answers "what
happened on this one incident?" and opens from any incident row. [Notifications](notifications.md)
is the account inbox, opened from the account menu.

Platform-wide model, spending, registration, and email settings are not in this menu. They live
under **Platform administration** → **Platform settings**, described in
[Platform administration](../operate/administration.md) and [Triage engine](../operate/triage.md).

## Appearance

Use the appearance icon at the bottom of the menu to choose System, Light, or Dark. The choice is
remembered per browser. Every screenshot in this guide is available in both, and follows the theme you have set
for this site.
